How We Work — Our Export Process

From first inquiry to final delivery, our streamlined B2B export process ensures transparency, reliability, and efficiency at every stage.

1

Inquiry & Quote

Send us your requirements — volume, product, destination — and receive a detailed quote within 24 hours.

2

Contract & Payment

We finalize terms (FOB/CIF/EXW), sign the export contract, and process your prepayment or L/C.

3

Sourcing & QC

Product is harvested, sorted, graded, and inspected under HACCP/ISO 22000 protocols.

4

Packaging & Docs

Export-grade packaging, phytosanitary certificates, and customs documentation prepared.

5

Logistics & Shipping

Cold-chain reefer transport via road, rail, or air to your port — with real-time tracking.

6

Delivery & Support

Product delivered to your warehouse. Post-delivery support for quality claims and reorders.

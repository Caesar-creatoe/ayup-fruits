About Ayup Fruits

Who We Are

Based in Uzbekistan — the ancient crossroads of the Silk Road — Ayup Fruits has been supplying premium-quality fruits and vegetables to importers across Europe, the Middle East, Russia, and Asia for over 15 years.

We work directly with local farmers, ensuring the freshest produce, competitive prices, and reliable export logistics. When you partner with Ayup Fruits, you are connecting with a tradition of excellence rooted in one of the world's most fertile regions.

Our Transport Fleet

We Deliver What We Sell

AyupFruits owns and operates its own transport fleet — so when you place an order, we handle the delivery ourselves. No third parties, no middlemen. Your fruits travel in our own trucks, under our direct control.

Refrigerated Trucks / Reefers

Our reefer trucks maintain a precise cold temperature throughout transit to protect the freshness and shelf life of every order. Your cherries, grapes, apricots and melons arrive in perfect condition — exactly as they left our farm.

Used for delivering our:
Fresh Cherries Grapes Apricots Pomegranates Figs Fresh Berries

Curtain-Side Trailers

For our packaged and non-refrigerated produce, we use curtain-side trailers. They allow efficient pallet loading and protect products from weather and damage during long international routes.

Used for delivering our:
Onions Potatoes Dried Fruits Nuts Packaged Melons

Standard Long-Haul Trucks

For bulk orders that don't require refrigeration, our standard trucks provide high-volume capacity for large consignments across Europe, the Middle East and Central Asia.

Used for delivering our:
Bulk Dried Fruits Walnuts & Almonds Garlic Large-Volume Orders

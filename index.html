🌍 Import Questions

Every import supplier undergoes a verification process before the first order. We conduct supplier audits, review certifications and arrange independent pre-shipment inspections by third-party surveyors.

🚢 Logistics & Payments

We offer Sea Freight (FCL/LCL reefer), Air Freight for premium/time-sensitive produce, Truck (Europe & CIS), Rail (China-Europe corridor) and Multimodal combinations. We select the optimal route for your delivery requirements.

Standard terms: 30–50% advance, balance against Bill of Lading. For established partners, LC at sight and open account terms are negotiable. We work with T/T, LC and escrow arrangements.

We can quote on all Incoterms: EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF, DAP and DDP. Our trade team will recommend the most cost-effective Incoterm based on your destination and requirements.

We aim to respond with a price offer within 24 hours for standard products. For custom sourcing requests, allow 2–3 business days. Contact us via WhatsApp for the fastest response.
